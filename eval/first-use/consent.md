# Consent (read aloud before starting)

Read this to the person word for word, then ask the question at the end.
Do not start until they say yes. You do not collect a signature, and you
do not write down their name anywhere in the study files.

---

Thanks for doing this. Before we start, here is what will happen and what
I will keep.

**What this is.** I am testing an app I built, not testing you. If
something is confusing, that is a problem with the app, and finding those
problems is the point.

**What I will do.** I will give you three short things to try, about
30 to 40 minutes in all. I will watch, keep time, and take notes on what
you do: for example, how long something took, where you got stuck, and
whether you finished. I will also ask you a few questions about the app.

**What you say to the app.** You will be talking to the app about your
day. You can talk about anything, make things up, or keep it vague. You do
not need to share anything real or private. Whatever you say is deleted
when we finish: the test account is wiped after the session, I will not
write down what you said, and nothing is recorded on audio or video.

**What I keep.** Only my notes on what you did, under a code like "P2",
never your name. These notes may be shown, as counts, to people
interested in my work, for example "2 of 4 people found the memory
screen".

**Quotes.** If you say something about the app that I would like to use,
I will read you the exact words after the session. I will only keep a
quote if you say yes to that exact wording. You can say no without giving
a reason.

**Stopping.** You can stop at any time, skip any task or question, and
you do not need to say why. If you stop, I will ask whether you want me to
throw away the notes from your session, and I will do what you say.

**Do you have any questions? Are you happy to go ahead?**

---

Facilitator: if they say yes, set `"consent": true` on the sheet. If they
say no, thank them and stop; record nothing.

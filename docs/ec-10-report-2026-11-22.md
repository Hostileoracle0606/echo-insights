# EC-10 report: Simulator, typed turns (22 Nov 2026)

**Build:** main after EC-09, plus the EC-10 fixes. Staging, iPhone 17 Pro
Simulator, routing on, `CONTEXT_REBUILD_MAX_TURNS=2` so a short session
rebuilds several times. **Driven by:** typed turns through the composer,
eight user turns. **Report:** `scripts/ec10-report.py` over the app's
`[voice]` log and the saved transcript.

## The script

1. My savings goal is 8k now and I'm trying to stick to it.
2. Work has been busy, we are launching the new dashboard on Friday.
3. My sister Priya is moving to Lisbon next month.
4. What's my savings goal right now?  *(exact question: fact layer)*
5. Honestly I'm a bit stressed about the launch.
6. Wait, you said 8k earlier. It's actually 7k, not 8k.  *(correction)*
7. Yes, that's right.  *(confirm)*
8. Anyway, the dashboard rehearsal went fine today.

## What a person noticed that the checks did not

- **Fixed during the run.** In the first attempt, turn 1 made Lumen call
  `correct_memory` on a plain statement and say it "didn't have the number
  stored". The tool and the prompt now say a value the user simply states
  is not a correction, and a "not found" result carries no spoken sentence.
  In the second run, turn 1 made no tool call.
- **Works.** Turn 4 was answered from stored facts ("eight thousand
  dollars"). Turn 6 got the read-back built in code ("So your savings goal
  amount is 7000 USD, not 8000 USD. Is that right?"). Turn 7 applied it:
  8000 retracted as `corrected`, 7000 stored `user_corrected`, and a
  correction rebuild followed. Turn 6's own "7k" was not recorded twice.
- **Still wrong.** After the last rebuild Lumen asked "How did Priya feel
  about it afterwards?" about the user's own dashboard rehearsal. Priya had
  nothing to do with it. This is a coherence miss that word-overlap checks
  cannot see. It is what the listening test is for, and a reason the gate is
  not passed yet.
- **Latency.** Plain turns after a rebuild stayed inside the normal range.
  Tool turns after a rebuild were slower than the normal tool p95 (2.1 s
  against 1.95 s), with two samples each.

## Go / no-go

**Not yet decided.** The automated half passes on this run: 0 boundary
failures in 3 rebuild turns, and plain post-rebuild latency inside the
normal p95. But the gate needs:

1. Spoken runs, Simulator and device. Typed turns skip speech endpointing,
   which is most of the real latency.
2. The blind listening test: reviewers mark where they hear a reset.
3. More rebuild turns than three, and the deep-history cases.

To run one: `scripts/ec01-rebuild-test.sh runtime`, then
`scripts/ec01-rebuild-test.sh sim 2` (or `device <id> 2`), speak, then
`scripts/ec10-report.py test-runs/<log> --out report.md`.
